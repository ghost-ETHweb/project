# **App Name**: Gallery Navigator

## Core Features:

- Header Navigation: Display a header with a clickable logo, main navigation links (Shops, Mall Map, For Partners, Contacts), a prominent 'Promotions' button, and search/favorite icons. Include a burger menu for mobile.
- Hero Slider: Implement a hero section with a large slider showcasing promotions, new stores, and events. Include buttons linking to 'View Shops,' 'Promotions,' and 'Mall Map.' Allow embedding of a video presentation.
- Shop Directory: Enable filtering shops by category (Clothing, Shoes, etc.), floor, and alphabetically. Present shop listings as cards with logos, names, categories, and floor locations. Enable a pop-up detail view with descriptions, photos, and promotions upon card click.
- Interactive Mall Map: Offer an interactive map of the mall with floor selection and shop locators. Mark restrooms, elevators, escalators, and parking areas. Allow searching by shop name.
- Partner Information: Provide a page detailing lease terms with a contact form for inquiries (name, contact info, business type, message), and a 'Download Brochure' button (PDF). Display contact details for the leasing department.
- Footer Information: Show a footer with the logo, a brief description, links to shops, services, partner info, contacts, social media icons, contact info (address, phone, email), and copyright/privacy policy information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Teal (#008080) for logos and accents, conveying sophistication and calm.
- Background color: Off-White (#F8F8FF) creates a soft and inviting backdrop.
- Accent color: Coral (#FF7F50) brings a vibrant and energetic feel.
- Headline font: 'Montserrat' (sans-serif) in bold/semi-bold. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body font: 'Open Sans' (sans-serif) in regular weight. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Simple, geometric icons that match the modern aesthetic of the website.
- Subtle transitions and parallax effects, particularly in the hero section.
